## Word data

- Phonetics, parts of speech and Chinese meanings come from
  [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), which is MIT-licensed.
- Exam membership headwords are kept in `data/verified/`. They are derived from
  public wordlists and textbooks named in `data/verified/README.md` and
  `docs/DATA.md`.
- The redistribution terms of those headword sources have **not** been
  independently cleared. The pack JSON and verified headword lists are included
  so the project is usable locally, not as a representation that the data is
  free to redistribute.
- If you republish the package or its data in another jurisdiction or channel,
  review the source terms first. A conservative alternative is to publish the
  code only and build packs locally from sources you are allowed to use.
